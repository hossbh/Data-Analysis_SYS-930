The notebooks

Two analyses

Graduate coursework in decision making via data analysis. The datasets are not included in the repository; the README lists the files each notebook needs.

01 · Census

New Jersey population by county

Descriptive statistics, a ranked bar chart, a distribution histogram and a choropleth map built by joining census data to county boundaries with GeoPandas.

Key result: county populations range from about 66 K to 905 K (Bergen County), with a median of about 449 K.
Open on GitHub → 02 · Housing

Texas housing market analysis

Data cleaning, distributions, yearly trends, price by year, city rankings, listing–sales relationships and a correlation heatmap.

Key result: shows how sales, volume and average price move together over time, and which cities are most and least expensive.
